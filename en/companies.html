Corporate yoga

Regular classes at your office, or a special part of your team day or company event.

Anyone who sits a lot feels it sooner or later in their neck and back. A yoga class brings movement into the working day, the breath slows down and the mind clears. Afterwards, people go back to their desks more relaxed.

I tailor the classes to the group, and no experience is needed. As a fixed weekly slot, yoga soon becomes something the team looks forward to. Online classes are possible too, but it is at its best when you are together in person.

Massage at your company

I bring my mats and give your employees massages right at the office.

It is the same massage as in my individual sessions: mindful bodywork drawing on Thai yoga massage, Shiatsu and other techniques. At its core, it works with the nervous system. Gentle touch, calm breath and a steady rhythm help people relax deeply, and tense muscles benefit too.

Everyone stays fully clothed and lies on a soft mat on the floor. For many, a massage day is a lovely reason to come into the office.

Massage workshop for teams

Thai yoga massage in pairs

We split into pairs, and I guide you step by step through Thai yoga massage. You take turns on the mat, so everyone gets to give and to receive. I show you each technique and movement and support you as you practise.

The focus is on feeling and on mindful touch. The body calms down, the parasympathetic nervous system switches into rest mode, and the flow of energy in the body is supported. Along the way, you get to see each other as a team in a new light.

No experience needed, just comfortable clothes and fresh socks.
