About me.

Lagom meets OM.

LagOHM combines the Swedish lagom – the art of just the right amount – with the sound of yoga. I'm Helena, raised in Sweden, and at home in Munich for many years now.

Movement has always made me happy, but it was yoga that stayed with me through thick and thin. The more digital and fast-paced the world became, the clearer my wish grew: real, mindful touch and breath – instead of screen time and distance.

To me, everything is energy – and when it can flow freely, there's room for balance and wellbeing. My work is to sense what your body needs right now, and help it find its flow again.

When I work, time and space seem to disappear, and it feels like a shared, peaceful meditation. The nicest compliment I ever received was from a client who, after their session, slept through the night for the first time in a very long while.

  • For anyone who sits a lot and wants to feel more mobile again
  • For anyone dealing with tension, or who hasn't slept properly in a while
  • For anyone who wants to reconnect with themselves in a digital everyday life – without any pressure to perform
Offer · Massage

Massage – arrive & let go

Mindful bodywork drawing on Thai yoga massage, Shiatsu and other techniques – at its core, it's less about the muscles than about your nervous system: gentle touch, calm breath and a steady rhythm help you arrive fully back in yourself. Tense muscles benefit too – but the focus is pure deep relaxation: pausing, recharging, simply being.

How a session unfolds

You stay fully clothed and lie on a soft mat on the floor. Gentle stretches, flowing movements, targeted pressure and touch come together into a treatment tailored entirely to you.

No fixed routine

I sense, listen, and follow what your body and your current state need. That's what makes each massage intuitive – and different every time.

At the centre: deep relaxation

A letting go on both a physical and mental level, creating space for recovery, calm and renewed balance.
