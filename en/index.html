About me.

Lagom meets OM.

LagOHM combines the Swedish lagom – the art of just the right amount – with the sound of yoga. I'm Helena, raised in Sweden, and at home in Munich for many years now.

I’ve always found joy in movement, but it was yoga that stayed with me through thick and thin. The more digital and fast-paced the world became, the clearer my wish became: real, mindful touch and breathing – instead of screen time and detachment.

To me, everything is energy – and when it can flow freely, there's room for balance and wellbeing. My job is to sense what your body needs right now, and help it find its flow again.

When I work, time and space seem to disappear, and it feels like a shared, peaceful meditation. The nicest compliment I ever received was from a client who, after their session, slept through the night for the first time in a very long while.

  • For all those who sit a lot and want to feel more mobile again
  • For anyone dealing with tension, or who hasn't slept properly in a while or struggles with sleep
  • For everyone who in this digital world wants to reconnect with their inner self – without any pressure to perform
Offer · Massage

Massage – relax & let go

Mindful bodywork drawing on Thai yoga massage, Shiatsu and other techniques – it's less about the muscles than about your nervous system: gentle touch, calm breath and a steady rhythm help you return to you. Tense muscles benefit too – but the focus is pure, deep relaxation: taking a time-out, recharging, simply being.

How a session unfolds

You stay fully clothed and lie on a soft mat on the floor. Gentle stretches, flowing movements, targeted pressure and touch come together into a treatment tailored entirely to you.

No fixed routine

I sense, listen, and follow what your body and your current state need. The massage is intuitive – and different every time.

The focus: deep relaxation

Letting go at both the physical and mental level, creating space for regeneration, peace and renewed balance.
